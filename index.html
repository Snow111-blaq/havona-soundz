Trust & transparency

Built on clear principles

Before public launch, these principles need to become complete, legally reviewed policies for the countries where Havona Soundz operates.

©️

Copyright & takedowns

Creators must have the rights or permissions needed for every recording, composition, sample, cover image, and uploaded file. A documented notice-and-review process is required.

🔐

Privacy & account security

Collect only necessary information, protect account data, provide account recovery, and never expose private artist or listener information. Owner access must be enforced server-side.

💿

Royalties & distribution

Streaming income is not a fixed amount per play. Actual payouts depend on platform terms, rights ownership, territories, distributor agreements, and reporting periods.

🤝

Community standards

No impersonation, fraudulent streams, unauthorized uploads, harassment, or misleading claims about royalties or distribution. Clear support and dispute routes should be available.
